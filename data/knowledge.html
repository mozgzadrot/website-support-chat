Getting Started

Welcome to Zephyr — the intelligent project collaboration platform built for modern teams. Getting up and running takes less than five minutes.

To create your account, visit app.zephyr.io and click Sign Up. You can register with your work email or use Google or GitHub single sign-on. Once registered, you'll land in the onboarding wizard which walks you through creating your first Workspace.

A Workspace is your team's private environment. Give it a name, invite your teammates by email, and choose a timezone — Zephyr uses this for deadline reminders and daily digest emails. After setup, you'll land on your Dashboard where you can create your first Project.

Projects contain Boards (Kanban-style columns), Docs (collaborative rich-text pages), and Goals (OKR tracking). You don't need to use all three — start with whatever fits your workflow best.

The iOS and Android mobile apps are available in their respective stores. Search for "Zephyr Project" to find the official app. Mobile apps sync in real time with the web version.

Pricing & Plans

Zephyr offers four plans to fit teams of every size.

Free — up to 3 users, 2 active projects, 1 GB storage, community support. No credit card required. The Free plan is a great way to evaluate Zephyr with a small team before committing.

Starter ($19 / seat / month) — unlimited projects, 10 GB storage per seat, email support with a 24-hour SLA, and access to Zephyr AI writing assistance (limited to 50 requests/month per user). Billed monthly or annually (save 20% on annual billing).

Pro ($49 / seat / month) — everything in Starter plus: unlimited AI requests, advanced analytics dashboard, custom fields on tasks, priority email support (4-hour SLA), and SSO via SAML 2.0. Minimum 5 seats.

Business ($149 / seat / month) — everything in Pro plus: dedicated Customer Success Manager, SLA-backed 99.9% uptime guarantee, SCIM provisioning, audit logs (90-day retention), and on-boarding training sessions. Minimum 20 seats.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. You can cancel or downgrade at any time from Workspace Settings → Billing. Refunds for annual plans are prorated from the cancellation date.

Non-profit and educational institution discounts of up to 50% are available — contact sales@zephyr.io with proof of status.

Integrations

Zephyr connects with the tools your team already uses. Native integrations are available for:

Slack — receive task assignments, deadline reminders, and @mentions as Slack notifications. You can also create Zephyr tasks directly from any Slack message via the /zephyr slash command.

GitHub & GitLab — link pull requests and commits to Zephyr tasks. When a PR is merged, the linked task automatically moves to the Done column. Supported on Starter and above.

Google Workspace — attach Google Docs, Sheets, and Drive files to any task. Single-click file picker, no manual URL copying required.

Microsoft 365 — same as Google Workspace but for OneDrive and SharePoint documents.

Zapier & Make — over 5,000 automation partners accessible via Zapier. Zephyr exposes trigger events (task created, task completed, comment added) and action steps (create task, update status).

REST API & Webhooks — full REST API with OpenAPI 3.0 documentation at docs.zephyr.io/api. Webhooks can be configured in Workspace Settings → Integrations. API keys are scoped per workspace and can be read-only or read-write.

Enterprise-grade integrations such as Salesforce, Workday, and custom SAML identity providers are available on the Business plan.

Security & Compliance

Zephyr treats data security as a first-class concern. Here is an overview of our security posture.

Encryption — all data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Encryption keys are managed through AWS KMS with automatic annual rotation.

Authentication — two-factor authentication (TOTP and hardware security keys via WebAuthn) is available on all plans and required by default on Pro and Business. SSO via SAML 2.0 and OIDC is available on Pro and above.

Compliance certifications — Zephyr holds SOC 2 Type II certification (renewed annually) and is compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and ISO 27001. A copy of our latest SOC 2 report is available to Business customers under NDA on request.

Data residency — data is stored in AWS us-east-1 by default. EU data residency (eu-west-1) is available on the Business plan at no additional cost.

Backups — automated daily backups with 30-day retention. Point-in-time recovery is available for Business customers.

Permissions — role-based access control with five levels: Owner, Admin, Member, Commenter, and Viewer. Guest access (external collaborators with view-only rights) is supported on Pro and above.

For a full security whitepaper or to request a pen-test report, contact security@zephyr.io.

Support

Zephyr offers several channels to get help, depending on your plan.

Help Center — docs.zephyr.io contains step-by-step guides, video walkthroughs, API reference, and a searchable FAQ. This is the fastest way to find answers for common questions.

Community Forum — community.zephyr.io is a peer-to-peer forum moderated by the Zephyr team. It's open to all users including Free plan. Great for workflow tips and feature requests.

Email Support — available on all paid plans at support@zephyr.io. Response SLAs: Starter 24 hours, Pro 4 hours (business days), Business 1 hour (24/7).

Live Chat — in-app live chat is available to Pro and Business customers during business hours (9 AM – 6 PM ET, Monday–Friday).

Dedicated CSM — Business plan customers are assigned a Customer Success Manager who provides quarterly business reviews, onboarding workshops, and proactive health checks.

To submit a bug report, click the ? icon in the bottom-left corner of the app and select Report a Bug. Please include your browser version and steps to reproduce — this helps us resolve issues faster.

Features

Zephyr combines task management, documentation, and goal tracking in one workspace so your team stops context-switching between tools.

Boards — flexible Kanban boards with drag-and-drop cards. Each card (task) supports subtasks, attachments, comments, custom fields, due dates, and assignees. Cards can be filtered by assignee, label, due date, or custom field value. Board templates for common workflows (Sprint, Bug Tracker, Content Calendar) are available out of the box.

Docs — a collaborative rich-text editor built into every project. Supports markdown shortcuts, inline code, tables, embeds (Figma, Loom, YouTube), and @mentions that notify teammates. Documents auto-save every 30 seconds and have a full version history.

Goals — set team or company OKRs and link Zephyr tasks as key result contributions. Goals roll up automatically as linked tasks are completed, giving leadership real-time progress visibility.

Zephyr AI — an AI writing assistant that helps draft task descriptions, summarise long comment threads, generate sub-task breakdowns from a high-level description, and suggest due dates based on team workload. Available on Starter (50 req/month) and unlimited on Pro and Business.

Reporting & Analytics — velocity charts, burndown charts, and workload heatmaps are available on Pro and above. Custom report builder with CSV export is a Business-only feature.

Time Tracking — start/stop timers on any task. Weekly time summaries can be exported to CSV or synced to billing tools via the API. Available on all paid plans.
